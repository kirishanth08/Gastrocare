You will drift into a peaceful sleep within seconds, breathe comfortably on your own, and wake up 10 minutes after completion with zero procedural memory and no grogginess.

Traditional hospitals often use ambient room air to inflate the colon, which can trap nitrogen gas and cause painful abdominal distension and cramps for hours afterward. GastroCare uses warm medical-grade carbon dioxide (CO2), which is naturally absorbed through tissue 150 times faster than air, virtually eliminating post-procedure gas and discomfort.

If you are 45 or older (or have a family history of colon polyps) and are otherwise generally healthy without severe cardiac or renal disease, you can complete our online triage assessment. Our medical leads verify your eligibility, and you proceed straight to your scheduled colonoscopy without taking time off for an initial office visit.

Immediately following your procedure, your gastroenterologist provides a color photo discharge report detailing whether any polyps were detected and removed. If tissue biopsies were collected, formal histological pathology is processed by our dedicated dermatopathology & GI lab and securely uploaded to your Patient Portal within 3 to 5 business days.
