WHAT TO EXPECT

YOUR DAY OF PROCEDURE WALKTHROUGH.

We eliminate procedural anxiety by making every step predictable, gentle, and transparent.

STEP 1
Arrival & Private Intake

Arrive 45 minutes prior. You are greeted in a private pre-op suite where vitals, IV access, and anesthesia consultation take place.

STEP 2
Gentle Sedation

You are wheeled to the sterile suite. You receive gentle propofol sedation and drift into a peaceful sleep in under 30 seconds.

STEP 3
Procedure & Polypectomy

Dr. Carter examines mucosal surfaces with HD 4K optics. Any polyps found are gently removed without any sensation or pain.

STEP 4
Comfortable Awakening

You awaken in recovery with a warm blanket. Dr. Carter reviews preliminary findings with you and your escort before discharge.
