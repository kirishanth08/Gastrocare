CLINICAL SAFETY

TRIAGE GUIDELINES: WHEN TO CALL VS. EMERGENCY CARE

Clear clinical protocols to help you determine the appropriate level of gastrointestinal medical attention.

Call Our On-Call GI Triage

Available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week

Contact our on-call gastroenterology nurse coordinator for any of the following procedural or clinical symptoms:

  • ✓ Severe nausea or inability to tolerate bowel preparation within 24h of scheduled colonoscopy.
  • ✓ Post-procedure fever over 101°F, chills, or persistent abdominal cramping not relieved by passing gas.
  • ✓ Acute worsening or moderate flare-up of diagnosed Crohn's disease or Ulcerative Colitis.
  • ✓ Urgent pre-op medication questions regarding blood thinners, anticoagulants, or insulin holds.
Call GI Triage Line: (206) 555-0199 →

Emergency Department Red Flags

Call 911 or go to nearest emergency hospital

Do not wait for outpatient clinic hours. Seek immediate emergency evaluation for any acute life-threatening symptoms:

  • ⚠️ Active, continuous, large-volume rectal bleeding (maroon or bright red blood with clots).
  • ⚠️ Vomiting frank blood or material resembling dark coffee grounds (hematemesis).
  • ⚠️ Severe, sudden, "board-like" abdominal rigidity with unmanageable, acute agony.
  • ⚠️ Sudden dizziness, confusion, clammy skin, or loss of consciousness (syncope).
Emergency Hotline: Call 911 Immediately
