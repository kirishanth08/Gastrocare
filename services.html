PATIENT PATHWAY

WHAT TO EXPECT: YOUR STEP-BY-STEP PROCEDURE JOURNEY

From preliminary intake to same-day discharge and nurse check-in, here is how our clinical team ensures seamless care.

1
Pre-Procedure Clinical Triage

Our registered triage nurse conducts a 15-minute phone review of your medical history, current anticoagulants or diabetes medications, and verifies insurance prior authorization.

Timeline: 7 to 10 days prior
2
Gentle Split-Dose Preparation

You receive our low-volume split-dose regimen (or pill-based Sutab option). Drinking half the evening before and half the morning of ensures pristine colon mucosal visibility.

Timeline: 24 to 12 hours prior
3
Private Suite Arrival & Check-In

Arrive with your adult companion escort. You will be settled into a private pre-op bay equipped with heated blankets, cardiac telemetry, and meet your physician and anesthesiologist.

Timeline: 45 mins before procedure
4
Gentle 15–25 Minute Procedure

You fall peacefully asleep under gentle monitored anesthesia. Your gastroenterologist performs high-definition mucosal imaging, removes any polyps, and takes targeted biopsies.

Timeline: 15 to 30 mins active duration
5
Direct Review & Photo Report

You wake up alert and clear-headed. Your gastroenterologist meets with you and your escort to review color high-definition procedural photographs and immediate preliminary findings.

Timeline: 20 to 30 mins recovery
6
Portal Histology & Follow-Up

Our triage team calls you the next morning to ensure complete recovery. Any pathology or histology biopsy reports are published to your secure patient portal within 48 to 72 hours.

Timeline: 24 to 72 hours post

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

COMMON QUESTIONS ABOUT OUR SERVICES & PROCEDURES

Clear, physician-vetted answers to common questions about sedation, bowel prep, recovery, and appointment scheduling.

No. At GastroCare, all endoscopic procedures are performed under Monitored Anesthesia Care (MAC) using propofol, administered by a board-certified anesthesiologist. You will be completely and comfortably asleep throughout the entire procedure, experiencing zero pain, gagging, or awareness. You wake up within 8 to 10 minutes feeling clear-headed without nausea or the grogginess of twilight sedatives.

Gone are the days of having to chug a gallon of unpalatable liquid in one sitting. We prescribe gentle, modern split-dose preparations (such as low-volume solutions or FDA-approved Sutab tablets). You consume the first portion the evening prior and the second portion the morning of your procedure. This split method is clinically proven to be substantially easier to tolerate and provides superior mucosal cleansing.

Because the lining of the colon does not have pain-sensing nerve endings, removing polyps (polypectomy) is 100% painless. Your doctor uses specialized micro-snares or cold forcep instruments to remove the polyp immediately during the colonoscopy. The tissue specimen is sent to our accredited pathology laboratory, and the histological analysis is reviewed with you within 48 to 72 hours.

Most PPO commercial insurance plans, Medicare, and self-pay patients do not require a referral and can self-refer directly through our online booking portal. If you have an HMO or specialized managed care plan, our billing concierge can assist you in obtaining the required referral from your primary physician within 24 to 48 hours.

Because we use medical-grade warm CO2 insufflation rather than room air, you will have negligible gas or bloating. You can eat a normal meal immediately upon leaving the endoscopy suite. Because of the mild sedation, federal regulations require having an adult escort drive you home, and we advise resting for the remainder of the day. You may resume normal driving, work, and exercise the following morning.
